2) Email setup

Platform pick: Klaviyo (free plan to start). Why Klaviyo over Mailchimp:

  • Klaviyo's free WooCommerce extension includes a built-in checkout consent checkbox, a newsletter signup form, and a "Started Checkout" trigger for abandoned-cart emails with a cart-rebuild link (Klaviyo Help Center, updated Oct 27, 2026).
  • Klaviyo Free covers 250 active profiles and 500 sends a month and includes automations. Mailchimp Free (250 contacts, 500 sends) does not include automation flows or email scheduling (mailchimp.com/pricing).
  • Mailchimp counts synced customers who never opted in toward its 250-contact cap.
  • Paid plans start at the same price: Klaviyo Email $20/mo for 500 profiles and 5,000 sends; Mailchimp Standard from $20/mo.

Setup steps (Forge, after Cory's APPROVED):

  1. Install Klaviyo for WooCommerce, connect the account, and let the historical order sync finish.
  2. Checkout opt-in: turn on "Add email marketing consent checkbox to your checkout page." Leave it unchecked by default. Label: "Email me Storm drops and deals."
  3. Newsletter signup: one Klaviyo signup form in the footer, plus an optional popup. Any offer on it (e.g. 10% off a first order, audit #4) needs Cory's decision.
  4. Abandoned cart (audit #5): flow triggered by Started Checkout. Email 1 at 1 hour, email 2 at 24 hours. Exclude anyone who placed an order. Free plan allows up to 3 live automations.
  5. Test order: confirm Started Checkout, Placed Order, and the opt-in all appear in Klaviyo.

List rules:

  • Build the list only from customers with at least one order, after Forge finishes the bot cleanup. None of the ~4,208 accounts without orders go on it.
  • Keep a separate segment of people who opted in (checkout box or signup form).
  • Compliance (CAN-SPAM): every email carries an unsubscribe link, a physical postal address, and an honest From line and subject. Mail only real past buyers and opt-ins. No bot accounts and no bought lists.
  • Treat the first send as a one-time holiday re-permission email ("Want to stay on the Storm list?"). After Cyber Monday, keep mailing only people who opted in or engaged.
  • Plan size: if real emailable customers are 250 or fewer, Free works. Above that, Email is $20/mo for up to 500. One email per month per profile fits Free; four holiday sends to 250 people need 1,000 sends, which means a paid month (see Costs).
